Yue Qian

The Chinese University of Hong Kong

I am a sociologist studying how gender, family, and work shape inequality and well-being, particularly in North America and East Asia.

I received my PhD in Sociology from The Ohio State University. My work has appeared in Nature Human Behaviour, American Sociological Review, Social Forces, and Journal of Marriage and Family.

Outside academia, I enjoy yoga, snowboarding, and windsurfing.
