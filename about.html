Yue Qian

Professor of Sociology
The Chinese University of Hong Kong

I am a Professor of Sociology at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. I received my PhD in Sociology from The Ohio State University and began my academic career at the University of British Columbia in 2016, where I advanced through the ranks to Full Professor in 2025. My research examines how gender, family, and work shape inequality and well-being, particularly in East Asia and North America.

I have published over 80 articles, including in Nature Human Behaviour, PNAS, American Sociological Review, Social Forces, and Journal of Marriage and Family, and delivered over 50 invited talks across North America, Asia, and Europe.

My research has been featured by The New York Times, The Washington Post, and The Economist, and cited in policy publications from the United Nations, World Bank, and World Health Organization.

Outside academia, I enjoy yoga, windsurfing, and snowboarding.
