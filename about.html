About

A sociological perspective

Yue Qian

The Chinese University of Hong Kong

I am a sociologist whose work examines gender, family, work, and inequality in global contexts, particularly North America and East Asia. I received my PhD in Sociology from The Ohio State University.

My research has appeared in journals including Nature Human Behaviour, American Sociological Review, Social Forces, and Journal of Marriage and Family.

Outside academia, I enjoy yoga, snowboarding, and windsurfing.
